Archie

A personal assistant, built for one person.

Archie is a private assistant that helps Jamin Burchard manage his calendar, email and schedule. It is not a product, has no users other than its owner, and is not available to sign up for.

It exists because coordinating a rotating shift schedule, a family calendar and a full inbox is genuinely hard, and the tools that already exist each solve one piece of it.
